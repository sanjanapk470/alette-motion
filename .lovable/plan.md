# Art portfolio redesign: editorial archive

## Direction
Rework the existing single-page portfolio into an artwork-first, immersive sequence inspired by Object & Archive’s large-scale editorial presentation. Follow the selected **Asymmetric staggered gallery**: a compact archive header followed immediately by four medium-led chapters with dramatically varied scale and alignment rather than a carousel.

## What visitors will see
- A night-museum palette with charcoal, pale ivory, muted gold, and soft sage; Cormorant Garamond headings paired with Karla body text.
- Four distinct presentations for the existing illustrative works, matching the selected direction: a prominent monsoon oil painting with offset title, a smaller Tanjore painting with vertical label, a full-width river watercolour, and an intimate framed miniature.
- Clear medium labels and artwork titles, with restrained movement as chapters enter view. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- An accessible way to browse by medium and open each painting’s existing details; retain the in-page detail dialog and previous/next navigation.
- A mobile layout that follows the chosen staggered composition closely, with a cohesive wider-screen arrangement.

## Technical approach
- Keep the portfolio on its current collection page with its local artwork record; do not add shopping, inventory, or new backend services.
- Update the page structure and semantic design tokens/styles to match the chosen palette, typography, and chapter composition. Reuse the existing artwork assets and factual details rather than the prototype’s invented titles, materials, or provenance.
- Check the finished page on desktop and mobile, including medium browsing, artwork opening, keyboard navigation, and layout/animation behavior.
